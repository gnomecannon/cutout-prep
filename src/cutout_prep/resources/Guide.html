Cutout Prep

Prepare leaf cutouts or masks for a group of scan photographs. Processing stays on your computer. The portable Windows app includes Python, its dependencies and the local segmentation model.

Scan one object — indoors or outdoors

  1. Open Scan masks. Choose your photos or their folder, then select a new project/output folder.
  2. Use Keep one object / target area. On the first useful photo, draw a box around the rock, log, plant or other subject. Include its whole outline, with a little room around it.
  3. Choose Preview mask. Green is kept; dark background is ignored. A Keep point restores part of the target. A Remove point tells the model that a turntable, hand, support or other region is background. Choose Preview again after refining.
  4. Choose Process group. The app uses SAM 2's temporal memory to follow the chosen subject through the photographs and predicts a new mask for each view. Keep photos in capture order. Processing uses the CPU so it can coexist with other GPU work.
  5. Use Next review to inspect views marked Review or Error. After a flip or large camera jump, select the target again on that view, preview it, then choose Process from this view. Approve a view only after its silhouette looks right. Approved masks are preserved on later batches. Uncertainty carries forward until a new anchor or approval, so a lost target cannot silently become a trusted sequence.
  6. Choose Export ready / approved photos. Import the resulting Prepared Photos folder into RealityScan. It contains byte-identical photo copies and matching photo.ext.mask.png masks. Enable masks for alignment and reconstruction in RealityScan.

Scan exports retain full photo framing, original camera metadata and original pixel dimensions. They are not square cutouts. White mask pixels are used; black pixels are ignored. Masks are written in the source file's pixel orientation, while the app displays them in the photograph's normal viewing orientation.

Larger outdoor scenes

Use Keep scene, remove a distraction to keep the scene and mask a selected unwanted region. Draw a box around that region; Remove points select it and Keep points protect nearby scenery. The app inverts that region into a reconstruction mask. Re-anchor when the unwanted region changes or leaves the frame. Do not use object isolation when the surrounding ground or cliff is part of the scan.

What still needs judgment

Different backgrounds are supported by the prompted segmentation model, rather than a fixed white-background rule. No model can reliably decide which connected ground belongs to your intended scan without a selection. Glass, reflections, thin foliage, very similar materials, occlusion and sudden view changes may need extra points. A high model score is not proof that the correct object was selected. Review important silhouettes before reconstruction.

Masking removes image regions; it cannot recover an unseen underside, supply missing overlap, repair alignment, remove baked-in lighting or guarantee a clean watertight mesh. Photograph the underside and enough shared side detail if you need a fully captured object.

Leaves and image cutouts

Use the Leaf & image cutouts tab for the established leaf processor. Pick an input folder, a separate output folder and a square texture size. This workflow crops, centers and resizes; it is appropriate for decals and cutout textures. It runs to completion and writes Preview.jpg and ProcessingReport.json. Existing shared Process Images.cmd workflows and leaf recipes remain available.

Save, resume and protect edits

A scan project saves its selections, masks, review state and original-file fingerprints in Scan Project.json. Open that file to continue. Original photos are never edited, renamed or deleted. Use a separate output folder for every independent scan. Exports that were edited by hand are preserved; an error reports the conflict instead of replacing them. If you paint an internal mask externally, inspect it in the app and approve it before exporting.

Cancel stops a scan batch after the current photo. Closing during a scan waits for that boundary. The leaf workflow finishes its current batch before closing. If a crashed app leaves a lock, first establish that no other app is using the project, then recover the lock; the app does not silently take it over.

If a previously exported view becomes excluded or needs review, its unchanged photo/mask copies move into Previous Exports. They no longer remain in the current import folder. Hand-edited old exports are preserved and require a separate output project before continuing.

Cliffs, scene edges and review

For a cliff or large surface that intentionally continues outside the photograph, enable Target may continue beyond photo edges before previewing or processing. Scene-removal selections may naturally touch the image edge. A framing warning applies only to that view; low-confidence tracking or abrupt target-size changes still hold following views until a new selection or approval. Review the actual silhouettes even when marked Ready.

Portable files and Mac

Keep the executable beside its _internal folder. You can move the complete portable folder or unzip it on another Windows computer; no Python installation or online account is required. The releases include a native Apple Silicon Mac app. Keep each platform’s complete application together. Intel Macs have not been validated. The source includes native build instructions; processing runs offline on the CPU.

Model and disclosure

Scan masking uses Meta SAM 2.1 Hiera Tiny, distributed under Apache-2.0. The app loads bundled safetensors weights locally, without remote model code. Leaf processing uses the established traditional image-processing routines. AI generated this utility's code and tests; automated checks and visual trials do not constitute an independent human code audit.
