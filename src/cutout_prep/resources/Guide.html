Cutout Prep

Prepare masks for 3D-scan photographs, or create leaf and artwork cutouts. Processing stays on your computer. Portable Windows and Apple Silicon Mac downloads include the local models and runtimes.

Photos for 3D scanning

  1. Choose Load photo folder.
  2. On the first useful photo, draw a box around what to keep. Include the whole rock, log, plant or selected surface, with a little room around it.
  3. Choose Process photos. The app follows your selection through the group. Keep photos in capture order. Ready photographs and masks save automatically.
  4. Inspect the outlines. Review photos finds flagged views. If a selection needs repair, open Adjust this selection, choose Click areas to keep or Click areas to remove, then Preview this photo’s result.
  5. Choose Use this photo’s result only after its outline looks right. This also updates saved results. After a flip or large camera jump, draw a new box and use More options → Process again from this photo to continue from there.
  6. Choose Show results folder. Import this Prepared Photos folder into RealityScan and enable masks for alignment and reconstruction.

A starting selection is needed because the app cannot know which connected rock, ground or background you intend to scan. Green shows what is kept; dark areas are ignored. White mask pixels are kept and black pixels are ignored. Original photos, framing, metadata and pixel dimensions are preserved.

Where results go

A new folder named Your photos - Scan Masks is created beside the photo folder. Loading the folder again creates a numbered folder and preserves earlier work. If the default location is not writable, the app asks where to save. To choose a location yourself, use More options → Load folder and choose save location. Choose a separate empty folder.

Progress saves automatically. To resume an earlier batch, choose More options → Open saved work and select its Scan Project.json. Project files contain local source paths and should remain private.

Cliffs and larger scenes

For a surface that intentionally continues outside the photograph, use More options → Mask settings → Subject continues beyond photo edges. This changes the framing check, not the accuracy of the outline.

To keep a scene while removing a selected distraction, choose More options → For the next photo group → Keep scene; remove selected area, then load the new folder. Draw around the unwanted region. Remove clicks select that region; Keep clicks protect nearby scenery. Reselect it when it changes or leaves the frame. Existing groups retain their original workflow.

Leaf and image cutouts

In Leaf & image cutouts, choose Load photo folder, then Process cutouts. Results save in a new Your photos - Cutouts folder beside the originals. Choose Show cutout results to inspect Preview.jpg and the processing report. More options contains image size and save-folder choices. The default is 4096 × 4096 pixels.

Detailed background removal is automatic. It predicts the subject before refining its boundary, protecting solid interiors while retaining predicted holes. Separate objects and small pieces are kept by default. Inspect fine gaps, hair, shadows and clutter in the preview. For unusual results, More options → Background removal method → Simple background (older method) selects the previous extractor.

Photo preparation also balances brightness and shadows. Clear stems can be turned downward; several separate subjects keep their capture orientation. Names such as Oak_Front.jpg and Oak_Back.jpg or Oak_Rear.jpg identify possible pairs. The rear is reflected into a shared front frame only when the outlines fit well. Weak matches keep independent shapes and are flagged; names do not establish specimen identity.

Use More options → Review leaf orientation and front/back pairs to inspect the last saved result, choose quarter-turns, assign unlabeled captures or keep views separate. Set a paired leaf’s front orientation; the fitted back follows. Apply choices and process again saves the choices and updates unmodified owned exports. Reopening the results folder reloads those choices. Hand-edited exports remain protected.

Saved image-settings.json recipes beside the photos are honored, including existing rotations, layouts and skipped views. Load saved leaf settings selects that photo-preparation format from another location. Earlier hand-retouched artwork is not recreated automatically from an untouched capture.

Lighting, automatic orientation, automatic pairing and keeping separate objects have switches in More options. Geometry analysis adds a preview pass, so large RAW captures can take minutes on the CPU. Final textures use the original capture, not preview pixels. The background model analyzes a 1024-pixel square, with edge refinement up to 2048 pixels on the long edge; tiny details can still be lost. Supplied transparency and explicit masks bypass automatic background removal. A limited recovery for incomplete predictions on uniform dark backing carries an extra review warning.

For already prepared images, choose Preserve existing artwork colors and alpha. This keeps supplied colors, translucent pixels and separate alpha pieces, and disables automatic photo cleanup, lighting and geometry. Explicit review choices and per-photo recipes can still be applied.

Fine colorful subjects on bright neutral paper can receive an automatic close-view pass to reduce backing left between branches. It takes extra CPU time and keeps the same folder/process workflow. Pale subjects and broad solid objects retain their existing route; inspect very small details and mixed materials.

A finishing pass can reduce small partly transparent paper rims around those fine edges. It protects near-opaque material and keeps the same workflow. Very fine pale details and reflections still need inspection.

LeafPreparation.json records decisions and saved choices. Like other reports, it contains local paths and should be checked before sharing.

This workflow crops, centers and resizes for decals and artwork. Use Photos for 3D scanning when the original photo framing must be preserved. Existing manually edited cutout exports remain protected.

Corrections, review and stopping

Adjust this selection includes drawing a new box, keep/remove clicks, undo, clearing a selection and previewing one photograph. More options contains outline adjustments and original/black-and-white photo display.

A framing warning affects that photograph. Uncertain subject tracking holds later views for review until a new selection or accepted result. A high model score is not proof of a correct outline. Inspect important results even when marked Ready. Skipped and unaccepted views are left out of the import folder.

Stop after current photo stops a scan batch without updating the saved results. Choose Process photos to run again. Closing waits for the current scan photo or cutout batch to finish. The cutout workflow runs to completion.

Originals are never edited, renamed or deleted. Edited exports are preserved; a conflict is reported instead of overwriting them. When an unchanged earlier export becomes skipped or held for review, it moves to Previous Exports so the current import folder stays consistent. If you paint an internal mask externally, inspect it in the app and accept it before saving results.

Limits

Supports, crossing wires, thin foliage, similar materials, reflections and occlusion may require corrections. Masks cannot recover an unseen underside, missing overlap, measured surface roughness or a complete watertight model. This app does not repair a reconstructed mesh or its glossiness.
